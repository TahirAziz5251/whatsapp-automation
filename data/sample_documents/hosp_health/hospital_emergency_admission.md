# City Healthcare & Hospital Emergency Admission & Trauma Protocol

## 1. 24/7 Emergency Care Overview
City Healthcare & Hospital maintains a state-of-the-art 24-hour Emergency & Trauma Center equipped with dedicated resuscitation bays, cardiac monitors, and emergency surgical operating suites.
- Location: Ground Floor, West Wing, Main Boulevard, Lahore.
- Emergency Ambulance Helpline: Call 1122 or hospital emergency line +92-42-35991122.
- Direct Walk-in: Patients requiring urgent medical care are received directly without prior booking or outpatient registration appointments.

## 2. Emergency Triage Categories
Upon arrival, patients undergo immediate clinical evaluation by senior triage officers:
- Red Category (Immediate Resuscitation): Severe cardiac arrest, major polytrauma, massive hemorrhages, severe respiratory distress. Care commences within zero minutes.
- Yellow Category (Urgent Acute Care): High-grade pediatric fever with seizures, moderate fractures, acute abdominal colic pain. Evaluation within 15 minutes.
- Green Category (Non-Urgent / Stable): Minor lacerations, chronic mild symptoms. Directed to OPD clinics.

## 3. Financial & Admission Guarantee Policy
Emergency life-saving stabilization is initiated immediately for all critical patients regardless of upfront payment or financial status. No advance deposit is required before commencing critical resuscitation.
