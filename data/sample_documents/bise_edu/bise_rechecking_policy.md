# BISE Board Examination Paper Rechecking Policy & Regulations

## 1. Rechecking Application Rules
Candidates dissatisfied with their Matric (Secondary School Certificate) or Intermediate (Higher Secondary School Certificate) examination marks may apply for official paper rechecking.
- Application Deadline: Within 15 calendar days from the official gazette announcement date.
- Rechecking Fee: PKR 1,200 per individual subject theory paper, payable via official bank challan at designated branches of Habib Bank Limited (HBL).
- Online Submission: Applications must be submitted through the official BISE online student portal with the 6-digit roll number.

## 2. Scope of Scrutiny & Recounting
According to BISE Board statutory regulations, paper rechecking strictly covers the following verification points:
- Verification that all answers in the candidate's answer script have been marked by the examiner.
- Recounting of total marks awarded across all questions to ensure error-free arithmetic summation.
- Verification that total marks on the answer script cover page accurately match the computerized result database.
- Examination scripts are NOT re-evaluated or re-marked by a new examiner under any circumstances.
