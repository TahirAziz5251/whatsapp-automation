# GlimsTech POS Hardware Warranty & Return Policy

## 1. Scope and Coverage
This official policy governs all retail point-of-sale hardware units supplied by GlimsTech POS Retail Automation across Pakistan.
- 80mm Thermal Receipt Printers: 12-month standard replacement warranty covering printhead and motherboard failures.
- Omnidirectional 2D Desktop Barcode Scanners: 24-month manufacturer defect replacement warranty.
- Heavy-Duty Steel Cash Drawers: 36-month mechanical warranty covering solenoid and slide rail assembly.
- All-in-One Touch POS Terminals: 12-month full hardware warranty including capacitive touch panel and power supply.

## 2. Claim Procedure & Replacement Window
Customers must report malfunctioning equipment within 14 calendar days of experiencing hardware failure.
The customer must provide the original serial number, invoice copy, and a brief description of the fault.
Replacement units are dispatched within 48 to 72 business hours upon verification of warranty eligibility.

## 3. Exclusions from Warranty
The warranty strictly excludes:
- Physical damage caused by drops, impacts, or liquid spills.
- Electrical damage resulting from severe voltage spikes without an approved surge protector.
- Unauthorized disassembly, third-party repairs, or tampering with tamper-evident warranty seals.
